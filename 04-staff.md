## CA Roles

The class will have <N> CAs organized in two roles ( <N> determined once the class site has settled )
* **Advising CAs** hold office hours (four hours per week per advising CA) and answer questions through ED in a timely fashion.  Questions related to homeworks should be directed to their OHs or to ED.
* **Grading CAs** grade homeworks, answer (mostly) grading-related questions on ED and hold OHs to answer such questions live.  Questions related to grading should be directed to them.

Across the entire CA team, we offer a mix of in-person and zoom OHs, spread throughout the week and at different hours of the day to serve a wide variety of student needs.  Times and locations are in the class calendar.
